Advanced comparison

Why no conventional R² for orthogonal regression? Ordinary OLS R² describes explained variation in the outcome under a vertical-error model. Orthogonal regression instead minimizes squared perpendicular distances (SSD). Its slope describes direction and steepness, and SSD measures remaining geometric error; neither is a standardized effect size by itself. Both depend on measurement units and scaling. Vertical SSE and perpendicular SSD assess different fitting criteria, and should not be treated as interchangeable.
